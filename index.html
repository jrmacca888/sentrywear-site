Why New Zealand, why now

Technology that's already standard practice offshore hasn't reached New Zealand's highest-risk sectors yet. That gap is the opportunity — and the longer it stays open, the more workers go without it.

A regulator actively pushing for it

WorkSafe New Zealand's new forestry Approved Code of Practice is driving fresh investment in compliance and monitoring technology across the sector.

No incumbent to displace

Unlike PPE vending, fixings, or packaging, connected worker safety has no established New Zealand distributor — this is a genuinely open category, not a crowded one.

Proven, not experimental

Every platform we work with is already deployed at scale in Australia, the UK, and North America — we're not asking New Zealand crews to be early adopters of unproven tech.
